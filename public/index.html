Vision service: checking…

Categories follow the 2017 ACC/AHA guideline: Normal <120/<80; Elevated 120–129/<80; Stage 1 130–139 or 80–89; Stage 2 ≥140 or ≥90; Crisis >180 and/or >120. BP4me is a logging aid, not a medical device. Always confirm the numbers against your monitor, and talk to a clinician about your readings.
